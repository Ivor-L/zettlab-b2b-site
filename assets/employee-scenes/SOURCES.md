# Employee scenario imagery

Generated on 2026-11-12 with the user-authorized Dola endpoint, using gpt-image-2 at medium quality, 1536×1024. Six independent generation requests.

Images are fictional illustrations of office situations, not photographs of actual employees, customers or client premises. On-slide captions identify them as AI generated. No user files or personal images were sent.

- logistics.webp: shipment follow-up in a warehouse office
- customers.webp: B2B customer inquiry and reply preparation
- content.webp: product video planning in a small studio
- finance.webp: order document and invoice review
- research.webp: competitor and customer feedback research
- keywords.webp: search trend analysis and advertising planning

Exact prompts are in PROMPTS.json. Original PNGs are saved locally in output/imagegen/employee-scenarios. WebP copies are used in employee-briefing.html. Credentials are not stored in this project.
